# 🌱 My Government Exam Journey

> **A Personal, Calm, No-Pressure Study Coach for Manikanta**

Designed specifically for a Diploma in Computer Engineering & B.Tech IT graduate with mathematics anxiety, beginner-level competitive preparation, and high potential when guided one step at a time.

---

## 🎯 Core Principles

1. **Remove Confusion** — No massive, scary syllabi on your screen.
2. **One Task at a Time** — The home screen shows **ONLY what you need to do today**.
3. **Easy → Medium → Hard** — No jumping into complex SSC-level questions before mastering the basics.
4. **Build a Strong Foundation Over Time** — Consistent, low-stress daily study beats erratic cramming.

---

## 🔁 The Golden Learning Loop

```
NxtWave (Learn) ➔ R.S. Aggarwal (Extra Practice) ➔ Assessment (Check) ➔ Revision (Remember)
```

- **NxtWave teaches me** (Primary Teacher: Video + Basic Exercise + MCQs + Assessment).
- **R.S. Aggarwal gives me extra practice** (Reasoning only: 10–15 questions per topic).
- **Assessment checks my understanding** (<60% = needs another explanation; 60–70% = almost there; >70% = understood 🎯).
- **Revision helps me remember** (Automatic spaced intervals: Day 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30).

## 🧭 Navigation & Features

| Tab | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🏠 Today** | Shows your current task, 5-step checklist, R.S. Aggarwal topic match, and daily study modes. |
| **📚 Subjects** | Complete breakdown of Phase 1 Reasoning (16 topics), Maths Without Fear (17 topics), General Studies (11 topics), AP Panchayat Secretary (25 topics), APPSC Endowment Officer, and SSC CGL. |
| **📝 Practice** | "Learn from Zero" reasoning breakdowns, 5-level tiered Maths progression, and interactive quiz drills with immediate step-by-step explanations. |
| **🔁 Revision** | Spaced repetition queue with structured 10-Minute Rapid Revisions (3 Rules + 3 Questions + 1 Mini Test). |
| **❌ Mistake Book** | Safe error log with smart diagnostic insights (e.g. calculation vs concept warnings). |
| **📊 Progress** | Visual progress percentage bars, weekly summaries, and **Next Week Priorities (Top 3 only)**. |
| **⚙️ Settings** | Ambient focus sounds (Gentle Rain / Brown Noise), Completion Chimes, Dark Theme, and JSON Data Backup/Restore. |

---

## ⚡ Daily Modes

- **Standard Day (2h 45m)**: Reasoning (45m) + Maths (45m) + GS (30m) + Panchayat (30m) + Revision (15m).
- **🛋️ I Am Tired Today (1h)**: Reasoning (20m) + Easy Maths (20m) + Light Revision (20m). *"It's okay to have a lighter day."*
- **⏱️ I Only Have 1 Hour (1h)**: Focused 1-hour session to keep momentum alive with zero guilt.
- **Missed a Day?**: *"Welcome back. Continue from where you stopped."* (No streak punishments).
